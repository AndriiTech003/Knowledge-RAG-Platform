# Welcome to Northwind - Your First Week

Welcome to Northwind Labs! We are glad you are here. This page walks you through your first week so you know what to expect, who to talk to and what you need to finish before Friday. If anything is unclear, ask your onboarding buddy or post in **#new-joiners** on Slack.

## Before your first day

- You will receive your laptop by courier (remote employees) or find it on your desk (office-based employees). Do not try to set it up before day one; IT will guide you.
- Your Northwind email address (firstname.lastname@northwind.example) is created five working days before your start date. The activation link for **Okta** is sent to your personal email the evening before you start.
- Your manager assigns you an **onboarding buddy**, a colleague from another team who will check in with you every day of the first week and every week after that for your first two months.

## Day 1 (Monday)

| Time (local) | What happens |
|---|---|
| 09:30 | Welcome coffee with your manager and buddy (office) or a video call (remote) |
| 10:00 | IT setup session: activate Okta, enrol in Okta Verify, set up 1Password and Slack (see the IT Setup Guide) |
| 11:30 | People team session: Workday profile, bank details, emergency contacts, benefits enrolment |
| 13:00 | Team lunch, paid by Northwind |
| 14:30 | Product tour of Atlas and Beacon with the Product team |
| 16:00 | Wrap-up with your manager: your 30-60-90 day plan |

New joiners start on a Monday, except when Monday is a public holiday, in which case they start on Tuesday.

## Days 2 to 4

- **Tuesday:** Northwind Security Basics course (about 90 minutes, required by POL-SEC-033) and anti-bribery e-learning. Meet your team's key stakeholders.
- **Wednesday:** "How Northwind works" session with the CEO, Maya Okafor, at 16:00 Lisbon time, held every Wednesday for all new joiners of the month. Maya covers our history, values and strategy and takes questions.
- **Thursday:** Department deep dive. Engineers get repository access and a first starter task; sales colleagues shadow customer calls; other teams follow the plan from their manager.

## Day 5 (Friday)

- Complete the **first-week checklist** in Workday (it lists every task on this page).
- Join the Friday **"Show & Tell"** at 15:00 Lisbon time, where teams demo what they shipped this week.
- Have a 30-minute check-in with your manager and give feedback on your onboarding in the short survey that Workday sends you.

## First-week checklist

1. Okta activated and Okta Verify enrolled
2. Laptop encrypted and showing as "compliant" in the device portal
3. Slack profile with photo, pronouns (optional), role and office
4. Workday profile complete: bank details, tax information, emergency contact
5. Security Basics and anti-bribery training completed
6. Read the Code of Conduct and acknowledge it in Workday
7. Read the Remote Work Policy and agree anchor days with your team
8. Book your first 1:1s with your teammates

We are happy you joined us. Take your time, ask lots of questions and enjoy your first week!
