# Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** HB-COC-01
**Owner:** Sofia Marques, Head of People
**Approved by:** Maya Okafor, CEO, and the Board of Directors
**Version:** 3.0, effective 1 July 2025

## A message from our CEO

"Northwind was built on trust: our customers trust Atlas to tell them the truth about their data, and we trust each other to do the right thing when nobody is watching. This Code describes what that means in practice. Read it, live it, and speak up when something feels wrong." - Maya Okafor

## 1. Who the Code applies to

The Code applies to all employees, contractors, interns and board members of Northwind Labs, in every country and at every company event, including offsites, conferences and online spaces such as Slack. Every employee acknowledges the Code in Workday during the first week and again every year in July.

## 2. Respect and inclusion

- Treat everyone with respect, regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age, disability, family status or any other characteristic.
- Harassment, bullying and discrimination are never acceptable. This includes unwelcome comments, jokes, images, physical contact and repeated exclusion.
- Use inclusive language. Use people's correct names and pronouns.
- Meetings are run in English unless everyone present speaks another language comfortably.

## 3. Acting with integrity

- Be honest in everything you say to customers, colleagues and partners. Never overstate what Atlas or Beacon can do.
- Record information accurately, including time, expenses and sales figures.
- Follow the Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy (POL-LGL-004). Never offer or accept anything that could influence a business decision.
- Respect competition law: never discuss pricing, bids or customer allocation with competitors.

## 4. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when your personal interests could influence, or appear to influence, your decisions at work. Examples:

- Holding more than 1% of the shares of a supplier, customer or competitor
- Hiring or managing a close relative or partner
- Working a second job for a competitor or customer
- Using Northwind information to benefit a personal investment

Declare any potential conflict in Workday under "Conflict of interest declaration" **within 30 days** of it arising. Most conflicts can be managed once they are declared; hiding one is a breach of this Code. Outside work for a non-competing business is allowed, but must be declared if it takes more than 8 hours per week.

## 5. Protecting information and assets

- Protect customer data and confidential information as described in the Information Security Policy.
- Use company assets responsibly and return them when you leave (see the Equipment Return Process).
- Do not trade in shares of any public company on the basis of non-public information you learned at Northwind.

## 6. Respecting customers' data

Customer data processed by Atlas belongs to our customers. Employees may access customer data only when it is necessary to provide support or operate the service, and only through approved tools with logging enabled. Browsing customer data out of curiosity is a serious breach of this Code.

## 7. Health, safety and wellbeing

Report unsafe conditions to your office manager. Never work under the influence of alcohol or drugs. Alcohol at company events is optional, and non-alcoholic options are always available.

## 8. Speaking up

If you see or suspect behaviour that breaks this Code, you have several options:

1. Talk to your manager, or to their manager if your manager is involved.
2. Contact the People team in #people-help or at people@northwind.example.
3. Use the confidential **speak-up portal** (speakup.northwind.example) or the speak-up hotline, which allow anonymous reports. See the Whistleblower and Speak-Up Policy (POL-LGL-002).

Northwind does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## 9. Investigations and consequences

Concerns are investigated promptly, fairly and confidentially by the People team or, for serious matters, by an external investigator appointed by the General Counsel. Employees are expected to cooperate. Breaches of this Code can lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal, and may be reported to the authorities.

## 10. Questions

When you are not sure what to do, ask yourself:

- Is it legal and in line with our policies?
- Would I be comfortable if it appeared in a newspaper or in #announcements?
- Does it reflect our values of Curiosity, Candour, Craft and Care?

If the answer to any of these is "no" or "not sure", stop and ask the People team before acting.
