# Offices and Locations

Northwind Labs has four offices. Lisbon is our headquarters; Berlin, Austin and Kraków are regional hubs. Employees who live too far from an office work remotely and are welcome to visit any office (book a hot desk in the Robin app at least one day in advance).

## Lisbon (headquarters)

- **Address:** Avenida da Liberdade 245, 4th floor, 1250-143 Lisboa, Portugal
- **Office hours:** Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00
- **Reception:** staffed 08:30 to 18:30
- **Office manager:** Inês Carvalho
- **Getting there:** Metro Avenida (blue line), 2 minutes on foot
- **Capacity:** 180 desks, 14 meeting rooms, a rooftop terrace and the all-hands auditorium "Tejo"
- **Parking:** 12 underground spaces reserved for visitors and employees with accessibility needs; bicycle storage on level -1

## Common rules for all offices

- Wear your Northwind badge visibly. Lost badges are reported to the office manager and replaced within one working day.
- Outside office hours, entry is possible with your badge, but you must tell the office manager in advance if you plan to work after 22:00 or at weekends, for safety reasons.
- Meeting rooms are booked through Google Calendar. Rooms that nobody has checked into on the room panel within 10 minutes are released automatically.
- Each office has an IT desk staffed on anchor days (Tuesday and Thursday) from 10:00 to 16:00. Outside these times, use #it-help.
- Visitors must be registered in advance; see the Guest Wi-Fi and Visitors page.
- Kitchens are stocked with coffee, tea, fruit and snacks. Please clean up after yourself; the last person out turns off the coffee machines.
