# Guest Wi-Fi and Visitors

This page explains how to register visitors to any Northwind Labs office and how guests can get online. It applies to customers, candidates, partners, friends and family visiting the Lisbon, Berlin, Austin or Kraków offices.

## Registering a visitor

1. Register your visitor in the **Envoy** app (or at envoy.northwind.example) **at least one working day before the visit**. Enter the visitor's full name, company, email address and the date and time of arrival.
2. Envoy emails the visitor a QR code and the office address the day before the visit.
3. On arrival, the visitor scans the QR code at the reception tablet, signs the visitor NDA on screen and receives a printed **visitor badge** with their name, the host's name and the date.
4. Reception notifies you in Slack and by email. **Collect your visitor at reception yourself**; visitors may not walk to meeting rooms alone.
5. Visitor badges are valid for one day only. Visitors return their badge at reception when they leave, and you are responsible for checking them out in Envoy.

Same-day visitors (for example a delivery that needs a signature) can be registered at reception by the office manager, but the host must still come down to collect them.

## Visitor rules

- Visitors must be accompanied by their host at all times, including in the kitchens and on the terrace.
- Visitors may not enter the IT room, the server cupboard or desks areas where Confidential information is visible on screens.
- Photographs inside the office are allowed only in the reception and meeting rooms.
- Groups larger than 10 visitors (for example a customer workshop) must be announced to the office manager at least 5 working days in advance.
- Candidates visiting for interviews are registered by the Recruiting team.

## Guest Wi-Fi

Visitors connect to the guest network **NW-Guest**. The network is isolated from the corporate network and only provides internet access.

- **Network name (SSID):** NW-Guest
- **Password:** printed on the visitor badge and shown on the screen at reception
- **Password rotation:** the NW-Guest password **rotates automatically every Monday at 06:00** local time
- **Bandwidth:** limited to 20 Mbit/s per device
- **Session length:** devices are disconnected after 12 hours and need to reconnect

Employees must never give visitors access to the corporate network **NW-Corp** or the employee network **NW-Staff**, and must not share their Okta credentials with anyone.

If a visitor needs a wired connection or more bandwidth for a demo, ask the IT desk at least two working days in advance.

## Deliveries and couriers

Parcels for employees are received by reception and stored in the mailroom. You receive an Envoy notification and can pick up parcels during reception hours. Personal deliveries are allowed but must be collected within 5 working days.
