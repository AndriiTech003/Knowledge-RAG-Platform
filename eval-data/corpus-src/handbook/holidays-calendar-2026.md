# Holidays Calendar 2026

This calendar lists the days on which each Northwind office is closed in 2026. Remote employees follow the public holidays of the country in their employment contract. Public holidays never count against your vacation allowance (see the Time Off and Leave Policy, POL-HR-002).

| Date | Day | Occasion |
|---|---|---|
| 22 May 2026 | Friday | Recharge Day |
| 18 September 2026 | Friday | Northwind Day (company anniversary) |
| 13 November 2026 | Friday | Recharge Day |
| 24 to 31 December 2026 | Thursday to Thursday | Year-end closure |

## Notes

- Holidays that fall on a weekend are not moved in Portugal or Germany.
- Customer-facing on-call duties during holidays are organised by each team; people working on a public holiday receive a replacement day.
