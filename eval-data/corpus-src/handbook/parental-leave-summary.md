# Parental Leave Summary

Northwind Labs offers the same parental leave to every parent, regardless of gender, and whether the child joins the family through birth, adoption, foster care or surrogacy. This page summarises the global minimum standard. Where local statutory leave is longer or better paid, the local rules apply, and Northwind tops up statutory payments so that you receive the benefits described here.

## How much leave

| Parent | Paid leave | When to take it |
|---|---|---|
| Primary caregiver | 20 weeks at full pay | Starting up to 4 weeks before the expected due date or placement |
| Secondary caregiver | 10 weeks at full pay | Within 12 months of the birth or placement, in up to 3 blocks |
| Parents of multiples (twins, triplets) | Additional 4 weeks for either parent | Together with the main leave |
| Pregnancy loss after 20 weeks | 6 weeks at full pay for the birthing parent, 2 weeks for the partner | Immediately |

To be eligible you need to have completed your probation period (normally 3 or 6 months, depending on the country). Employees still in probation receive statutory leave only.

## Before your leave

1. Tell your manager and the People team at least **12 weeks** before the expected start of your leave, or as soon as you can for adoption and surrogacy.
2. Submit the "Parental leave request" in Workday with the expected dates. You can change the dates later.
3. Agree a handover plan with your manager. Your manager arranges cover; you are not expected to find a replacement yourself.
4. Book a confidential "pre-leave" conversation with your People partner to go through local paperwork (for example Elterngeld in Germany or the subsídio parental in Portugal).

## During your leave

- You keep all benefits, including health insurance, pension contributions and your learning budget.
- Vacation days continue to accrue in full.
- You can choose up to **10 "keeping in touch" days** (for example to attend an offsite) without ending your leave. They are fully voluntary.
- Your laptop and accounts remain active, but you are not expected to read Slack or email.

## Coming back

- **Phased return:** for the first 4 weeks after returning, you can work 60% of your normal hours at full pay.
- Your role, level and team are protected while you are on leave.
- Lactation rooms are available in all four offices; ask your office manager for access.
- Flexible schedules after your return are discussed with your manager and documented in Workday.

## Questions

Ask the People team in #people-help (or privately at people@northwind.example). The full parental leave rules for each country are available in Workday under "Benefits" > "Family".
