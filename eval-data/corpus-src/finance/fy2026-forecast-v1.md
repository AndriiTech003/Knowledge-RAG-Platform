# FY2026 Financial Forecast (Version 1)

**Version:** 1.0 (original annual plan)
**Prepared by:** FP&A team
**Approved by:** Daniel Reyes, CFO, and Maya Okafor, CEO
**Date:** 20 January 2026
**Board review:** 28 January 2026

## Summary

This is the original FY2026 forecast for Northwind Labs, built bottom-up from the sales capacity model and the product roadmap for Atlas and Beacon. It sets the company targets for the fiscal year 1 January – 31 December 2026.

The headline target for FY2026 is an **ending ARR of $48M** (forty-eight million US dollars) at 31 December 2026, compared with $35.1M at the end of FY2025. This represents 37% ARR growth.

## Key targets

| Metric | FY2025 actual | FY2026 target (v1) |
|---|---|---|
| Ending ARR | $35.1M | $48.0M |
| Total revenue | $33.2M | $43.5M |
| Gross margin | 76.0% | 78.5% |
| Net revenue retention | 112% | 116% |
| Operating margin | -11.0% | -4.0% |
| Year-end cash | $36.2M | $29.0M |

## Quarterly ARR path (v1)

| Quarter end | Target ARR |
|---|---|
| Q1 2026 | $38.4M |
| Q2 2026 | $41.6M |
| Q3 2026 | $44.6M |
| Q4 2026 | $48.0M |

## Key assumptions

- New logo bookings grow 30% year over year, driven by the expansion of the EMEA team in Berlin.
- Atlas 5 general availability in June 2026, enabling a price uplift of 8% on new Enterprise contracts.
- Beacon attach rate on new Enterprise deals of 45%.
- Gross revenue retention of 92%.
- Cloud hosting unit costs fall 6% after the reserved-capacity migration.

## Risks

- A delay of Atlas 5 would shift Enterprise price uplift into the second half.
- EMEA hiring delays could reduce new logo capacity.
- Foreign exchange exposure on euro-denominated payroll.

## Revision policy

The forecast is re-issued after the close of each half-year if actual results deviate from plan by more than 3% on ARR. A revised version supersedes this document in full.
