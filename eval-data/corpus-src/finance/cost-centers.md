# Cost Center List 2026

This list defines the cost centers used in NetSuite, Expensify and Workday for fiscal year 2026. Every purchase order, expense report and employee record must carry exactly one cost center. The list is maintained by the Controller, Elena Petrova; changes are made only at the start of a quarter.

## How to use cost centers

- Use the cost center of the team that **benefits** from the spend, not the team that places the order.
- Shared costs (office rent, company-wide software such as Slack, Google Workspace, Okta and Notion) are booked to CC-4800 and allocated monthly by headcount.
- If you are unsure which cost center to use, ask in #finance-ops before submitting.
