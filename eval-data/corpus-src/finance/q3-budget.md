# Q3 2026 Operating Budget

**Owner:** Elena Petrova, Controller
**Approver:** Daniel Reyes, Chief Financial Officer
**Period covered:** 1 July 2026 – 30 September 2026
**Status:** Approved

## 1. Purpose

This document records the approved non-payroll operating budget for the third quarter of fiscal year 2026 (Q3 2026) for every department of Northwind Labs. Payroll, benefits and employer taxes are planned separately by the People team and are not part of these figures. All amounts are in US dollars and exclude VAT.

The Q3 budget is the second quarterly re-plan under the FY2026 operating plan. It reflects the actual results of Q2 2026 and the revised full-year forecast (version 2). Department heads may not commit spend beyond their approved quarterly envelope without a budget transfer request, as described in section 6.

## 3. Planning assumptions

The Q3 envelope was built on the following assumptions, agreed with the executive team on 26 June 2026:

- Quarterly revenue of $10.6 million, consistent with the revised FY2026 forecast (version 2).
- Non-payroll operating expense held below 20% of quarterly revenue.
- No new office leases; the Kraków office expansion is deferred to 2027.
- Cloud hosting is budgeted as cost of revenue and is excluded from department envelopes.
- An unallocated contingency of $60,000 is held centrally by the CFO and released only on written request.
- Exchange rates fixed for planning at 1 EUR = 1.09 USD and 1 PLN = 0.25 USD.

## 4. Key changes versus Q2 2026

- **Marketing** increased its program spend to fund the Atlas 5 launch campaign in September and two field events in Berlin and Austin.
- **Engineering** moved two tooling renewals (observability tooling and load testing) into Q4, lowering its Q3 envelope compared with Q2.
- **Sales** added budget for the EMEA partner enablement program, partly offset by lower travel spend.
- **IT & Security** includes the annual SOC 2 Type II audit fee, which is paid in Q3 every year.
- **People** includes the autumn employer-branding campaign and the Lisbon summer internship cohort.

Spend outside these themes requires the approval path described in section 6.

## 6. Budget controls and transfers

- Budget owners can see their remaining envelope in the NetSuite budget-versus-actual report, refreshed every Monday.
- Transfers between lines within the same department up to $25,000 can be approved by the department head.
- Transfers between departments, or any increase of a department envelope, require a budget transfer request approved by the Controller (Elena Petrova) and the CFO (Daniel Reyes).
- Purchases still follow the purchase approval thresholds defined in the Procurement Policy (POL-FIN-005), regardless of available budget.
- Unspent Q3 budget does not roll over to Q4 unless the CFO approves a carry-forward in writing before 30 September 2026.

## 7. Reporting

FP&A will publish a mid-quarter budget-versus-actual review on 14 August 2026 and a final Q3 variance report as part of the September month-end close. Variances above 10% or $20,000 per department, whichever is lower, must be explained by the department head in the variance report.
