# Q2 2026 Financial Results

**Prepared by:** FP&A, reviewed by Elena Petrova (Controller)
**Presented by:** Daniel Reyes, CFO, to the executive team on 21 July 2026
**Period:** 1 April 2026 – 30 June 2026

## 1. Executive summary

Northwind Labs closed the second quarter of 2026 with total revenue of **$10.14 million**, up 5.8% from Q1 2026 and 27% year over year. Annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached **$41.8 million** at 30 June 2026, an increase of $2.2 million in the quarter. Gross margin improved to **78.4%**, helped by the migration of the Atlas ingestion tier to reserved cloud capacity in May.

Net revenue retention for the trailing twelve months was 114%, and gross revenue retention was 92%. Beacon, the alerting and on-call add-on, was attached to 38% of new Atlas Enterprise deals in the quarter.

Operating loss narrowed to $0.61 million (6.0% of revenue) from $0.88 million in Q1 2026. Cash and cash equivalents at quarter end were $31.4 million.

## 2. Highlights

- 64 new logos signed, including 11 Enterprise customers.
- Largest new contract: a three-year Atlas Enterprise agreement with a German logistics company, $640,000 annual contract value.
- Logo churn of 1.9% in the quarter, the lowest since 2024.
- Days sales outstanding (DSO) improved from 52 to 47 days.
