# FY2026 Financial Forecast (Version 2, Revised)

**Version:** 2.0 (mid-year revision)
**Prepared by:** FP&A team
**Approved by:** Daniel Reyes, CFO, and Maya Okafor, CEO
**Date:** 15 July 2026
**Supersedes:** FY2026 Financial Forecast (Version 1), dated 20 January 2026

## 1. Summary

This revised forecast replaces version 1 of the FY2026 forecast in full. Version 1 should no longer be used for planning, quota setting or board materials.

The FY2026 ending ARR target is revised from $48M to **$46.5M** at 31 December 2026. The reduction of $1.5M reflects the four-week slip of Atlas 5 general availability from June to July 2026 and a lower-than-planned Beacon attach rate in the first half. ARR at the end of Q2 2026 was $41.8M, slightly ahead of the version 1 path for Q2 ($41.6M), but the second-half bookings outlook is lower.

Total revenue for FY2026 is now forecast at **$42.4M**, and the gross margin target is raised to **79.0%** because of hosting savings achieved earlier than expected.

## 4. Changes in assumptions

- Atlas 5 general availability moved to 21 July 2026; the Enterprise price uplift applies from Q3 only.
- Beacon attach rate on new Enterprise deals lowered from 45% to 38%, in line with first-half actuals.
- New logo bookings growth lowered from 30% to 24% year over year.
- Hosting unit costs fell 9% (plan: 6%) after the reserved-capacity migration in May.
