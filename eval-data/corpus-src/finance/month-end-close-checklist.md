# Month-End Close Checklist

**Owner:** Elena Petrova, Controller
**Applies to:** Finance Operations, FP&A, and budget owners
**Target:** close the books by business day 6 (BD6) after month end

Northwind Labs closes its books monthly in NetSuite. The close calendar counts business days (BD) after the last calendar day of the month, using the Lisbon public holiday calendar. Quarter-end months (March, June, September, December) follow the same checklist with the additional steps in the last section.

## Before month end (BD-3 to BD0)

1. Remind budget owners in #finance-announcements to confirm goods receipts for open purchase orders in Ramp.
2. Expensify: remind employees that expense reports submitted after BD0, 18:00 Lisbon time, are included in the following month's close.
3. Confirm the payroll files for Portugal, Germany, Poland and the US have been received from the People team via Workday.
4. Freeze vendor master data changes from BD-1 until BD3.

## BD1

1. Run the revenue recognition schedule for Atlas and Beacon subscriptions in NetSuite.
2. Import Salesforce closed-won bookings and reconcile with billing.
3. Book payroll journal entries for all four entities.
4. Download bank statements and start bank reconciliations.

## BD2

1. **Accruals deadline:** budget owners must submit accrual requests for goods or services received but not yet invoiced by **BD2 at 12:00 Lisbon time** using the accrual form in Notion. Requests received later are booked in the following month.
2. Book prepaid expense amortization (software subscriptions, insurance).
3. Book fixed asset depreciation.

## BD3

1. Complete bank reconciliations for all accounts; unreconciled items over $1,000 must be explained.
2. Book intercompany charges between Northwind Labs Lda (Portugal), Northwind Labs GmbH (Germany), Northwind Labs sp. z o.o. (Poland) and Northwind Labs Inc. (US).
3. Revalue foreign currency balances at month-end rates.

## BD4

1. Reconcile all balance sheet accounts above $10,000. Each reconciliation needs a preparer and a reviewer.
2. Review deferred revenue roll-forward against the billing system.
3. FP&A prepares the preliminary budget-versus-actual report by cost center.

## BD5

1. Controller review of the trial balance and flux analysis (month-over-month variances above 10% and $25,000).
2. Budget owners receive their budget-versus-actual report and have 24 hours to comment.

## BD6

1. Controller sign-off and period lock in NetSuite.
2. The CFO (Daniel Reyes) receives the monthly management pack.

## Additional quarter-end steps

- Physical count of hardware inventory in each office.
- Revenue cut-off testing on a sample of 25 invoices.
- Quarterly management results presentation prepared for the CFO by BD10.
- Audit committee pack prepared by BD15 for the external auditors.
