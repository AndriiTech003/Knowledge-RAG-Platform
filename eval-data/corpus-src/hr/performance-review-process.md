# Performance Review Process and Rating Scale

**Owner:** Sofia Marques, Head of People
**Audience:** People team and people managers (HR Confidential)
**Tool:** Workday Performance module

## Overview

Northwind Labs runs two performance cycles per year: a mid-year review (developmental, no rating change to pay) and a year-end review (rated, linked to the annual compensation cycle). Both cycles cover all permanent employees who joined at least 90 days before the cycle start date.

## 2026 calendar

| Step | Mid-year 2026 | Year-end 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Self-assessment opens in Workday | 1 June 2026 | 2 November 2026 |
| Peer feedback (3–5 peers) | 1–12 June 2026 | 2–13 November 2026 |
| Manager assessment due | 19 June 2026 | 20 November 2026 |
| Calibration sessions | 23–26 June 2026 | 30 November – 4 December 2026 |
| Review conversations completed | 10 July 2026 | 18 December 2026 |

Calibration sessions are run per department and chaired by a People Partner. VP-level calibration for Engineering is chaired by Priya Natarajan and for Sales by Tom Lindqvist.

## Rating scale

Northwind uses a five-point rating scale at year end:

| Rating | Label | Description | Guideline distribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Exceptional | Consistently far exceeds expectations; a role model across the company | up to 5% |
| 4 | Exceeds | Regularly exceeds the expectations of the role and level | 15–20% |
| 3 | Meets | Fully meets the expectations of the role and level | 60–70% |
| 2 | Partially meets | Meets some expectations; improvement needed in specific areas | 5–10% |
| 1 | Does not meet | Does not meet expectations; a performance improvement plan is required | up to 3% |

The guideline distribution is applied at department level for groups of 25 or more employees. It is a guideline, not a forced curve.

## Performance improvement plans (PIP)

- An employee rated 1 ("Does not meet") is placed on a PIP of 60 days.
- An employee rated 2 twice in a row is placed on a PIP of 60 days.
- The PIP is written by the manager with the People Partner and reviewed at 30 and 60 days.
- Possible outcomes: successful completion, extension by up to 30 days, or termination following local employment law in Portugal, Germany, Poland or the US.

## Promotions

Promotion nominations are submitted together with the year-end manager assessment. Requirements:

- At least 12 months at the current level.
- A rating of 4 or 5 in the current cycle.
- A written case showing the employee already operates at the next level for at least two quarters.

Promotions are approved by the department VP and the Head of People and take effect on 1 March together with merit increases.

## Confidentiality

Individual ratings are visible only to the employee, their manager chain and the People team. Ratings must never be discussed in public Slack channels or stored outside Workday.
