# Compensation Bands 2026

**Owner:** Sofia Marques, Head of People
**Approved by:** Maya Okafor (CEO) and Daniel Reyes (CFO) on 27 January 2026
**Effective:** 1 March 2026 (annual compensation cycle)
**Classification:** HR Confidential. Do not share outside the People team.

## 1. Principles

- Northwind Labs pays base salary within a band defined per job family, level and location zone. Bands are reviewed once a year against benchmark data (Radford and Ravio, 50th to 65th percentile).
- The midpoint represents a fully competent employee at that level. New hires are normally offered between the band minimum and the midpoint.
- Offers above the midpoint require approval by the Head of People; offers above the band maximum require approval by the CEO.
- Bands are expressed as annual gross base salary. Sales roles show on-target earnings (OTE) separately.

## 3. Levels

Engineering levels run from L1 (Associate) to L7 (Principal). Go-to-market levels run from S1 to S5. Managers use M1 to M4.

## 6. Annual cycle 2026

- Merit budget: 3.5% of base payroll; promotion budget: 1.0%.
- Increases are effective 1 March 2026 and are processed in Workday.
- Employees rated "Exceeds" are eligible for up to 8% merit; "Meets" for up to 4%; "Partially meets" for 0%.
