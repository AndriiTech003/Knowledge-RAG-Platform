# Employee Relations Case Handling Procedure

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Procedure ID | HR-PRO-007 |
| Owner | Sofia Marques, Head of People |
| Audience | People Partners and the People leadership team |
| Case system | Workday Case Management (restricted to the People team) |
| Last reviewed | 30 March 2026 |

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how the People team receives, investigates and closes employee relations (ER) cases at Northwind Labs, including complaints of harassment, discrimination, bullying, conflicts between colleagues, and concerns raised about a manager. It complements the public Code of Conduct, which describes how employees can raise a concern.

## 2. Intake

Concerns can arrive by email to people-confidential@northwind.example, directly to a People Partner, through a manager, or through the anonymous Speak Up hotline.

- Every concern is logged in Workday Case Management within **1 business day**, with a case ID in the format ER-2026-NNN.
- The person raising the concern receives a written acknowledgement within **2 business days** of receipt.
- Cases are classified as Low, Medium or High severity at intake.

| Severity | Examples | Investigation lead | Target closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Interpersonal conflict, minor policy questions | People Partner | 15 business days |
| Medium | Repeated conflict, manager behavior concerns, unfair treatment | Senior People Partner | 30 business days |
| High | Harassment, discrimination, retaliation, threats, fraud | Head of People plus external investigator | 45 business days |

High-severity cases are reported to the CEO (Maya Okafor) within 24 hours, without names unless strictly necessary. Cases involving a member of the executive team are escalated to the Chair of the Board.

## 3. Investigation

1. Agree terms of reference: scope, allegations, investigator and timeline.
2. Interview the complainant, the respondent and witnesses. Each person may bring a colleague or, where local law provides, a works council or union representative (for example Berlin works council members).
3. Collect evidence: Slack messages, emails, documents. Access to an employee's Slack or Google Workspace data requires written approval from the Head of People and the Head of Security (Jonas Weber).
4. Write an investigation report with findings on each allegation: substantiated, not substantiated, or inconclusive.

Interim measures such as changing reporting lines or paid leave can be applied during the investigation; they are not a disciplinary sanction.

## 4. Outcomes

- Outcomes may include no action, coaching, a written warning, a final written warning, or termination in line with local employment law.
- Both parties are informed of the outcome in writing. The complainant is told whether the complaint was substantiated but not the details of any sanction.
- Retaliation against anyone who raised a concern in good faith is itself a High-severity case.

## 5. Records and confidentiality

- ER case files are retained for 6 years after case closure, then deleted.
- Only the People team and the investigator have access to case files.
- Quarterly anonymized ER statistics (number of cases by severity and closure time) are shared with the executive team.
