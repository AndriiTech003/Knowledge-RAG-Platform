# 2026 Headcount Plan

**Owner:** Sofia Marques, Head of People, with FP&A
**Approved by:** Maya Okafor (CEO) on 22 January 2026; mid-year update approved on 20 July 2026
**Classification:** HR Confidential

## 1. Summary

Northwind Labs started 2026 with 402 employees. The original plan approved in January targeted 468 employees at 31 December 2026 (66 net new hires). At the mid-year update the target was lowered to **455 employees**, a net increase of 53 over the year, to align with the revised FY2026 forecast. As of 30 June 2026 the company had 421 employees.

Hiring is prioritized for Engineering (Atlas 5 and Beacon reliability) and EMEA sales capacity in Berlin. Backfills for regretted attrition are approved automatically; all other requisitions require approval in Workday by the department VP and the Head of People.

## 4. Hiring rules for the second half

- Requisitions not opened by 30 September 2026 are moved to the 2027 plan.
- Kraków expansion hiring is frozen until 2027; backfills only.
- Contractors converting to employees count against the department target.
- Attrition target: below 12% voluntary attrition (trailing twelve months: 10.8% as of June 2026).

## 5. Open priorities

1. Engineering: 6 senior engineers for Beacon on-call reliability, 4 for Atlas ingestion.
2. Sales: 5 Account Executives in Berlin for the DACH and Nordics territories.
3. Customer Success: 3 Technical Account Managers for Enterprise customers.

Recruiting status is reviewed every two weeks in the People leadership meeting.
