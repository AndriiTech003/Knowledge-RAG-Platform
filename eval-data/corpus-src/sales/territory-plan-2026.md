# 2026 Territory Plan (EMEA and North America)

**Owner:** Tom Lindqvist, VP Sales
**Prepared with:** Revenue Operations
**Effective:** 1 February 2026 – 31 January 2027

## 1. Principles

- Territories are assigned by the customer's headquarters country (account billing country in Salesforce), not by where the buyer sits.
- Named Enterprise accounts (more than 2,000 employees) are assigned to Enterprise AEs; all other accounts belong to the Commercial team of the territory.
- Existing customers stay with their current AE for expansion unless the account moves segment.
- Territory disputes are resolved by the regional Sales Director within 5 business days; if the directors disagree, Tom Lindqvist decides.

## 5. Rules of engagement

- SDRs are pooled per region (NA and EMEA) and route meetings to the AE who owns the account.
- Inbound leads from countries without a named AE go to the Commercial round-robin of the region.
- Partner-sourced deals are registered in the Salesforce partner portal and stay with the territory AE; the partner is compensated as defined in the Partner Program.
- An AE may not work an account in another territory without written approval from both Sales Directors.

## 6. Review cadence

Territory coverage and named-account lists are reviewed by Sales Directors at the end of each half-year. Changes take effect on 1 August and 1 February. RevOps publishes the updated account assignment in Salesforce one week in advance.
