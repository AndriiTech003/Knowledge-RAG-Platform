# Competitor Battlecard: Sentinel Data and Lumen Observe

**Owner:** Product Marketing with Sales Enablement
**Last refreshed:** 8 June 2026
**Use:** internal only. Never share this document with customers or partners.

## Competitive landscape

In 2026 Atlas meets two competitors in most data observability evaluations:

- **Sentinel Data**, a US-based vendor focused on large enterprises, strong in financial services.
- **Lumen Observe**, an open-core tool popular with engineering-led startups and mid-market companies.

In the first half of 2026 Sentinel Data appeared in 46% of competitive Enterprise opportunities and Lumen Observe in 31% of Growth tier opportunities.

## Sentinel Data

### Where they are strong
- Long list of enterprise connectors, including mainframe sources.
- Brand recognition with banks in NA East.
- Mature role-based access controls.

### Where we win
- Time to value: Atlas monitors are live in under one day with automatic baselines, while Sentinel deployments typically take 6–10 weeks with professional services.
- Built-in alerting and on-call with Beacon; Sentinel requires a third-party paging tool.
- EU data residency with hosting in Frankfurt, which Sentinel does not offer.

### Landmines to set
- "How long did your last tool take to go from contract to first useful alert?"
- "Who will maintain the rules when your data changes every week?"

### Typical objection and response
- *"Sentinel has more connectors."* Ask which connectors are actually required. Atlas covers the warehouses, lakes and orchestration tools used by more than 90% of our pipeline, and custom sources are possible with the Atlas SDK.

## Lumen Observe

### Where they are strong
- Free open-source tier and a strong developer community.
- Flexible checks written as code.

### Where we win
- Total cost of ownership: self-hosting Lumen requires an engineering team to maintain it.
- Machine-learning anomaly detection out of the box; Lumen only supports hand-written thresholds.
- SOC 2 Type II certified SaaS with SSO via Okta, SAML and SCIM included in Enterprise.

### Landmines to set
- "Who on your team will be on call for the observability tool itself?"
- "How do you detect problems you did not write a check for?"

## Win/loss summary H1 2026

| Competitor | Competitive deals closed | Win rate | Top loss reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sentinel Data | 58 | 47% | Incumbent relationship with bank IT |
