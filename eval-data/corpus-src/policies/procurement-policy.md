# Procurement Policy

**Policy number:** POL-FIN-005
**Owner:** Elena Petrova, Controller
**Approved by:** Daniel Reyes, CFO
**Effective date:** 1 March 2026

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines how Northwind Labs buys goods, software and services from suppliers, who approves a purchase and which terms we agree to. It applies to every purchase paid by Northwind except employee expense claims, which follow the Expense Policy (POL-FIN-001), and travel bookings, which follow the Travel Policy (POL-TRV-002). How Finance Operations onboards vendors and pays invoices is described in the Vendor Onboarding and Payment Procedure (FIN-PRO-002).

## 2. How to buy

- Every purchase starts as a purchase request in **Ramp Procurement**. Ask in #finance-ops if you do not have access.
- The request names the supplier, the amount including tax, the cost center and a short business justification.
- Finance Operations raises the purchase order (PO) only after all approvals in section 3 are complete. Do not sign an order form or contract before the PO exists.
- **No PO, no pay:** invoices without a valid PO number are returned to the vendor.
- The purchase must fit the approved budget of the cost center. The CFO contingency cost center CC-4900 cannot be used for purchase orders directly.
- New software tools also need an IT review in #it-help before the request is approved, so that they can be connected to Okta single sign-on.

## 3. Approval thresholds

Amounts are the total contract value including tax; for multi-year contracts the value of all years counts.

| Total purchase value | Required approvers |
|---|---|
| Up to $5,000 | Cost center owner |
| $5,000.01 to $25,000 | Cost center owner and department VP |
| $25,000.01 to $100,000 | Cost center owner, department VP and the Controller |
| Above $100,000 | Cost center owner, department VP, the Controller and the CFO |
| Above $500,000, or any contract longer than three years | All of the above and the CEO |

You may not split one purchase into several requests to stay under a threshold. Approvers may not approve purchases from a supplier in which they have a personal interest; such interests are declared under the Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy (POL-LGL-004).

## 4. Competitive quotes

- Purchases above $25,000 need **at least three written quotes**, attached to the Ramp request.
- Purchases above $100,000 are run as a formal request for proposal (RFP) by the Procurement team.
- A single supplier may be chosen without quotes only with a single-source justification approved by the Controller (for example, renewals of an existing platform or a unique technical capability).

## 5. Supplier terms

- Default payment terms are **net 30 days** from receipt of a valid invoice. Shorter terms need the Controller's approval.
- For contracts above $100,000 Procurement tries to negotiate net 45 or net 60 terms.
- Prepayments of more than 25% of the contract value need the CFO's approval.
- Contracts with auto-renewal must be entered in the Ramp renewal calendar, with a reminder at least 60 days before the notice deadline.

## 6. Suppliers that handle data

Suppliers that will process customer or employee personal data need a completed Security vendor risk assessment and a data processing agreement before the PO is raised. Suppliers with access to Restricted data must meet the certification requirements of the Information Security Policy (POL-SEC-037).

## 7. Exceptions and breaches

Exceptions to this policy are approved in writing by the CFO. Purchases made without the required approvals may be cancelled, and the cost may be charged back to the department; repeated breaches are handled under the disciplinary process.
