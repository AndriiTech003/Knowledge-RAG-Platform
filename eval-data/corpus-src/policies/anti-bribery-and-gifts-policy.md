# Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy

**Policy number:** POL-LGL-004
**Owner:** Legal team, with Elena Petrova (Controller) for the gifts register
**Effective date:** 1 November 2025

## Our position

Northwind Labs has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption. We win business because Atlas and Beacon are good products, never because of improper payments or favours. This policy applies to every employee, contractor, reseller and agent acting on Northwind's behalf, and it reflects the UK Bribery Act, the US Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and the anti-corruption laws of Portugal, Germany and Poland.

## What counts as a bribe

A bribe is anything of value offered, promised, given, requested or accepted to improperly influence a business decision. "Anything of value" includes cash, gift cards, meals, travel, event tickets, job offers for relatives, donations and discounts that are not available to everyone.

Facilitation payments (small payments to speed up routine government actions such as customs clearance) are prohibited, even where they are common local practice.

## Gifts and hospitality

Modest gifts and hospitality are a normal part of business relationships. They are acceptable when they are reasonable, infrequent, transparent and not given during an active tender or contract negotiation.

| Situation | Limit | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Giving a gift to a customer or partner | $100 per recipient per year | Claim under the Expense Policy and log it in the gifts register |
| Receiving a gift | $75 per gift | Log any gift over $75 in the gifts register within 5 working days |
| Receiving a gift worth more than $250 | Not allowed to keep | Return it politely or hand it to the People team for the annual charity raffle |
| Hospitality offered to a public official | Any amount | Needs prior written approval from the Legal team |
| Cash or cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers) | Never allowed | Decline, in both directions |

The gifts register is a Google Form linked from the Finance page in Notion. Elena Petrova, Controller, reviews the register every quarter.

## Public officials

Employees of government agencies, public universities, public hospitals and state-owned companies count as public officials. Because Northwind sells Atlas to several public-sector customers, any hospitality, travel or gift for a public official requires prior written approval from Legal, regardless of value.

## Third parties

Resellers and agents must pass a due-diligence check before they are appointed and must sign our Partner Code of Conduct. Commissions must be reasonable, documented in a contract and paid only to a bank account in the partner's own name and country.

## Charitable donations and sponsorships

Donations made in Northwind's name require approval from the CFO, must go to a registered charity and must never be made at the request of a customer or public official during a deal.

## Training and reporting

All employees complete the anti-bribery e-learning module during onboarding and every two years afterwards. Sales and partnership staff complete it every year. Concerns can be raised through the speak-up channels described in the Whistleblower and Speak-Up Policy (POL-LGL-002).

## Consequences

Breaching this policy can lead to dismissal and to criminal penalties for individuals. Partners who breach it will have their agreements terminated.
