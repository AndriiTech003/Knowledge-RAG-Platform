# Acceptable Use Policy

**Control ID:** POL-SEC-003 · **Owner:** Jonas Weber, Head of Security · **Effective:** 1 February 2026

This policy describes how Northwind Labs systems, devices, accounts and networks may be used. It complements the Information Security Policy and applies to employees, contractors and interns.

## 1. Company devices

- Your Northwind laptop is for work. Limited personal use (checking personal email, online banking, reading news during breaks) is acceptable as long as it does not affect your work or the security of the device.
- Do not let family members, friends or anyone else use your work laptop.
- Do not install software from outside the Kandji Self Service catalogue (macOS) or the Company Portal (Windows) without approval from IT Operations.
- Do not jailbreak, root or remove device management from any company phone or tablet.

## 2. Accounts and identity

- Use your Northwind Google account only for Northwind business. Do not use it to sign up for personal services.
- Never share your Okta credentials or let someone else approve an Okta Verify push for you.
- Shared accounts are only allowed when a system does not support individual users, and the credentials must be stored in a shared 1Password vault.

## 3. Generative AI tools

Employees may use the company-approved AI assistants listed in Notion under "Approved AI tools". Do not paste Confidential or Restricted data (as defined in POL-SEC-009) into any AI tool that is not on that list. Always review AI-generated output before you rely on it or share it with customers.

## 4. Email, chat and social media

- Do not forward company email automatically to a personal mailbox.
- Be professional in Slack and email: assume that anything you write could be read by a customer or a regulator.
- When posting on social media about the industry, make clear that the opinions are your own. Only the Marketing and Communications team speaks officially for Northwind.
- Never share customer names, roadmap details or internal metrics on social media without approval from Marketing.

## 5. Prohibited activities

The following are never allowed on Northwind systems or networks:

1. Accessing, storing or sharing illegal, sexually explicit, hateful or harassing material.
2. Mining cryptocurrency or running any personal commercial activity.
3. Scanning, probing or testing systems you do not own, unless it is part of an approved security test.
4. Bypassing security controls, including VPN, endpoint protection or web filtering.
5. Using peer-to-peer file sharing software or downloading pirated content.
6. Connecting personal storage devices or unmanaged network equipment to office networks.

## 6. Monitoring

Northwind logs access to its systems and may review logs when investigating a security incident or a suspected policy violation. Monitoring is proportionate and carried out in line with local law and works council agreements, in particular in Germany. Private content is not reviewed unless required for an investigation approved by the Head of Security and the Head of People.

## 7. Office networks

Employees connect to the **NW-Corp** network with their Okta credentials. Personal phones may use the employee network **NW-Staff**. Visitors use the separate guest network described in the handbook; employees must not share NW-Corp credentials with visitors.

## 8. Reporting and enforcement

Report suspected misuse in #security-help or to security@northwind.example. Violations may lead to the loss of access rights and to disciplinary action.
