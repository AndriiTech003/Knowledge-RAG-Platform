# Whistleblower and Speak-Up Policy

**Policy number:** POL-LGL-002
**Owners:** Sofia Marques, Head of People, and the Legal team
**Effective date:** 1 October 2025

## Why this policy exists

Northwind Labs wants everyone to feel safe raising concerns about wrongdoing. This policy explains what you can report, how to report it, how reports are handled and how you are protected. It implements the EU Whistleblower Directive (2019/1937) and the national laws in Portugal, Germany and Poland, and it also applies to our US employees.

## What you can report

You can report any genuine concern about wrongdoing related to Northwind's business, for example:

- fraud, bribery or corruption, including breaches of the Anti-Bribery and Gifts Policy (POL-LGL-004)
- breaches of data protection rules or misuse of customer data
- financial irregularities or accounting manipulation
- health and safety risks
- discrimination, harassment or retaliation
- environmental damage
- any attempt to conceal one of the above

Personal grievances about your own employment terms (for example a disagreement about a promotion decision) are normally handled through your manager or the People team, not through this policy.

## How to report

You can choose any of these channels:

1. **Speak-up portal (EthicsPoint):** available 24/7 at speakup.northwind.example, in English, Portuguese, German and Polish. You can report anonymously; the portal gives you a case key so you can follow up without revealing your identity.
2. **Speak-up hotline:** call +351 800 210 517 (Portugal), 0800 181 2240 (Germany), 800 112 4410 (Poland) or 1-855-430-0917 (US). The hotline is run by an independent provider.
3. **In person or by email:** contact the Head of People (Sofia Marques) or the General Counsel at legal@northwind.example.
4. **External authorities:** you may also report directly to a competent national authority.

## What happens after you report

| Step | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement of receipt | Within 7 days |
| Initial assessment by the Speak-Up Committee | Within 14 days |
| Feedback to the reporter on actions taken | Within 3 months |

The Speak-Up Committee consists of the General Counsel, the Head of People and an independent board member. A committee member who is involved in a report is excluded from handling it.

## Protection against retaliation

Northwind does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded. Retaliation includes dismissal, demotion, negative performance reviews, exclusion from projects and harassment. Anyone who retaliates faces disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.

Knowingly making a false report is a disciplinary offence.

## Confidentiality and records

The identity of a reporter is shared only with the people who need it to investigate, unless the reporter agrees otherwise or the law requires disclosure. Reports and investigation files are kept for 5 years after the case is closed and then deleted.

## Questions

Questions about this policy can be sent to legal@northwind.example. The policy is reviewed every two years by the Legal team.
