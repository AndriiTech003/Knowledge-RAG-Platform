# Expense Policy

**Policy number:** POL-FIN-001
**Owner:** Elena Petrova, Controller
**Approved by:** Daniel Reyes, CFO
**Effective date:** 1 March 2026

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains which business expenses Northwind Labs reimburses, the limits that apply and how to claim them. Travel-specific rules (flights, hotels and per diems) are covered by the Travel Policy, POL-TRV-002. Where the two documents overlap, the Travel Policy takes precedence for travel costs.

## 2. General rules

- Expenses must be necessary for Northwind business, reasonable and supported by a receipt.
- Every expense is claimed in **Expensify**. Paper forms and email claims are not accepted.
- Submit expenses **within 30 days** of the date the cost was incurred. Claims older than 90 days are rejected unless the Controller approves an exception.
- An itemised receipt is required for every expense of **$25 or more** (or €25 / 100 PLN). For smaller amounts a short description is enough.
- You may not approve your own expenses, and you may not split a single purchase into smaller claims to stay under a limit.
- Expenses are reimbursed in your payroll currency with the next monthly payroll run after approval.

## 3. Approval

| Claim amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $1,000 | Direct manager |
| $1,000.01 to $5,000 | Direct manager and department VP |
| Above $5,000 | Department VP and Controller |

Purchases of software, services or equipment from suppliers are not personal reimbursements; they need a purchase order raised by Finance Operations (ask in #finance-ops) and are not covered by this policy.

## 4. Corporate cards

Employees who travel more than six times per year, and all managers in Sales, may request a Northwind corporate card (issued by Ramp). Corporate card transactions sync to Expensify automatically and must have a receipt attached within 7 days. Personal purchases on a corporate card are not allowed, even if you intend to repay them.

## 5. Reimbursement limits

The table below lists the standard limits. Amounts are in USD; employees paid in EUR or PLN use the equivalent shown in Expensify, which is updated every January.

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Client meals and entertainment | $90 per person | Includes tax and tip; list all attendees and their companies |
| Team meals (internal only) | $40 per person | Max one per month per team; needs manager pre-approval |
| Working lunch with a candidate | $35 per person | Record the candidate name in the claim |
| Home office equipment | $600 one-time | Desk, chair, monitor, headset; first 6 months of employment |
| Home internet | $45 per month | Remote and hybrid employees only |
| Mobile phone plan | $50 per month | Only for roles on the eligible list maintained by Finance |
| Co-working space | $250 per month | Fully remote employees with no Northwind office within 50 km |
| Professional memberships | $400 per year | e.g. IEEE, ACM, CFA Institute |
| Late-night taxi home | $60 per trip | After 21:00 when work requires staying late |
| Gifts to customers | $100 per recipient per year | Must also follow POL-LGL-004 (Anti-Bribery and Gifts) |

Books, courses and conference tickets are paid from your personal learning budget (see the Learning Budget page in the handbook), not from these categories.

### 5.1 Alcohol

Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of a client meal or an approved team celebration, and never above two drinks per person. Spirits bought at retail are never reimbursed.

## 6. Expenses that are not reimbursed

- Personal clothing, grooming and toiletries
- Fines, penalties and parking tickets
- Personal subscriptions (streaming, news, fitness) unless listed in section 5
- Gifts to colleagues paid personally (use the People team's celebration process instead)
- Annual fees of personal credit cards and currency conversion fees above 3%
- Charitable donations made in Northwind's name without approval from the CFO

## 7. Lost receipts

If you lose a receipt for an expense of $25 or more, complete the "Missing receipt declaration" in Expensify. Missing-receipt declarations are limited to three per calendar year. After that, expenses without receipts are not reimbursed.

## 8. Audits and misuse

Finance audits a random 10% sample of expense reports every month, plus every report over $5,000. Submitting a false or inflated claim is treated as gross misconduct. Reimbursements paid in error are recovered from the next payroll.
