# Internal Communication Guidelines

Northwind Labs works across four offices, two continents and up to nine time zones. Good written communication is what keeps us fast. These guidelines explain which tool to use for what, the most important Slack channels and the response times we expect from each other.

## Which tool for what

| Need | Tool |
|---|---|
| Quick questions, team chat, announcements | Slack |
| Decisions and documentation that must last | Notion |
| Work tracking (engineering, product, IT) | Jira |
| Customer communication | Email (Gmail) and Salesforce |
| Meetings | Google Meet (default) or Zoom for external webinars |
| Formal HR matters (contracts, leave, benefits) | Workday |

**Rule of thumb:** if someone will need to find it in three months, it belongs in Notion, not only in Slack. Slack messages are deleted automatically after **18 months**.

## Key Slack channels

| Channel | Purpose |
|---|---|
| #announcements | Company-wide news from leadership and the People team. Read-only; only the leadership team and People team can post. |
| #general | Company-wide conversation and questions for everyone |
| #new-joiners | Questions and introductions from people in their first months |
| #it-help | Laptop, accounts, access requests and other IT support |
| #security-help | Reporting phishing, lost devices and other security concerns (monitored 24/7) |
| #people-help | Questions about Workday, leave, benefits and policies |
| #ask-finance | Expenses, Expensify and invoices |
| #travel-desk | Travel booking and Navan support |
| #office-lisbon, #office-berlin, #office-austin, #office-krakow | Office logistics, lunches and events |
| #til | "Today I Learned": share interesting things you learned |
| #kudos | Thank and recognise colleagues |
| #random | Everything else (pets, music, memes that respect the Code of Conduct) |

Channels for teams follow the pattern **#team-<name>** (for example #team-platform); channels for projects follow **#proj-<name>** and are archived when the project ends. Use **#ext-<customer>** for Slack Connect channels shared with customers, and never post internal information in them.

## Response times

| Channel | Expected response |
|---|---|
| Direct message or team channel during working hours | Within 4 working hours |
| Email from a colleague | Within 1 working day |
| Email from a customer | Within 1 working day, even if only to acknowledge |
| @here or @channel mentions | Use only for urgent matters affecting the whole channel |

Nobody is expected to reply outside their working hours. Use Slack's "Schedule send" when writing to colleagues in other time zones, and set your working hours and time zone in your Slack profile.

## Meetings

- Default meeting length is **25 minutes** or **50 minutes** to leave time between calls.
- Every meeting invite needs an agenda. No agenda, no meeting.
- **Focus Fridays:** no internal recurring meetings on Friday mornings (before 13:00 local time).
- Record important decisions in Notion within one working day and link them in the relevant channel.
- All-hands takes place on the first Thursday of every month at 16:00 Lisbon time (10:00 Austin time) and is recorded for those who cannot attend.

## Status and availability

- Set your Slack status when you are on vacation (🌴), sick (🤒), travelling (✈️) or in focus time.
- Keep your Google Calendar up to date; colleagues use it to find meeting slots.

## Tone

Be kind, be clear and assume good intent. Write so that someone in another team, in another country, reading on their phone, can understand you. Disagree openly in the channel rather than in private messages, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
