Learning Budget

At Northwind Labs, Curiosity is one of our four values. To support it, every employee has a personal learning budget to spend on courses, books, certifications and conferences that help them grow.

How much you get

  • Every full-time employee receives $1,500 per calendar year (or €1,400 / 6,000 PLN for employees paid in those currencies).
  • Part-time employees receive a pro-rated amount.
  • New joiners receive the full amount in their first year if they start before 1 July, and half the amount if they start on or after 1 July.
  • Unused budget does not roll over into the next year.
  • In addition to the money, every employee can take 3 learning days per year to attend training or conferences without using vacation days.

What you can use it for

Eligible Not eligible
Online courses (Coursera, O'Reilly, Udemy, Frontend Masters) General degree programmes (see "Larger programmes" below)
Professional books and e-books Hardware, laptops or tablets
Conference tickets and workshops Travel to a conference already paid under the Travel Policy
Certification exams (AWS, GCP, CKA, PMP, CFA) Hobby courses unrelated to your role or career at Northwind
Language classes for English, Portuguese, German or Polish Gym memberships and personal coaching outside work topics
Coaching and mentoring programmes approved by your manager Software subscriptions for personal use

Travel and accommodation for a conference are not paid from the learning budget; they are booked and claimed under the Travel Policy (POL-TRV-002) and need manager approval as a business trip.

How to use it

  1. Discuss the learning activity with your manager in your next 1:1 if it costs more than $300 or takes place during working hours.
  2. Pay with your corporate card if you have one, or pay personally.
  3. Claim the cost in Expensify using the category "Learning & Development" within 30 days of purchase. Attach the receipt and a one-line description of what you will learn.
  4. Your remaining balance is shown in Expensify under "Budgets".

Larger programmes

For longer programmes (for example a part-time master's degree or an executive course) above your annual budget, you can apply for education sponsorship. Applications are reviewed twice a year, in March and September, by the Head of People and your department VP. Sponsorship may include a requirement to stay with Northwind for 12 months after completion.

Sharing what you learn

After a conference or course, we encourage you to share a short summary in #til ("Today I Learned") on Slack or present at the Friday Show & Tell. Teams that share what they learn make everyone better.

Questions

Ask in #people-help, or contact the Learning & Development lead, Marta Sousa.
