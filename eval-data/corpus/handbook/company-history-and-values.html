Company History and Values

Where we come from

Northwind Labs was founded in 2017 in Lisbon by Maya Okafor and Rui Fonseca. Both had spent years as data engineers at large retailers, where broken data pipelines regularly went unnoticed for days and led to wrong decisions. Their idea was simple: data should be monitored as carefully as servers are. They started the company in a shared co-working space in Lisbon's LX Factory with four colleagues and a whiteboard.

Milestones

Year Milestone
2017 Company founded in Lisbon on 18 September; first prototype of Atlas built
2018 First paying customer, a Portuguese e-commerce company; team grows to 15
2019 Atlas 1.0 released with the Starter and Growth tiers; Berlin office opens
2020 Moved fully remote during the pandemic; launched the Enterprise tier
2021 Austin office opens to serve North American customers; team passes 150 people
2022 Beacon, the alerting and on-call add-on for Atlas, launched in public beta
2023 Kraków office opens; Beacon becomes generally available
2024 ISO 27001 certification; Atlas reaches 1,000 customers
2025 Headquarters move to Avenida da Liberdade in Lisbon; team reaches around 400 people
2026 Around 420 employees in four offices and more than 20 countries

Rui Fonseca served as CTO until 2022 and now advises the company as a board member. Priya Natarajan joined as VP Engineering in 2022.

Every year on 18 September, the anniversary of the founding, all offices close for Northwind Day. The day starts with a short talk from Maya, followed by local team activities and a dinner in each office city.

Our mission

Help every company trust its data. Atlas tells data teams when their data is late, incomplete or wrong, and Beacon makes sure the right person hears about it at the right time.

Our values

We have four values. We use them in hiring, in performance conversations and when we make hard decisions.

Curiosity

We ask why, we experiment and we keep learning. We would rather admit that we do not know something than pretend that we do. Everyone has a learning budget because we believe curious people build better products.

Candour

We say what we think, kindly and directly, and we expect the same from others. We share information openly inside the company, including bad news. Disagreement is welcome; disrespect is not.

Craft

We take pride in our work and sweat the details, whether it is code, a customer email or an expense report. "Good enough" is a decision we make consciously, not a habit.

Care

We care about our customers, our colleagues and the communities we work in. We look after each other's wellbeing, we make room for life outside work and we leave places better than we found them.

The Northwind name

The name comes from the north wind that blows over the Tagus estuary in Lisbon in summer, known locally as the nortada. It keeps the city cool and the sky clear. We want to do the same for our customers' data.
