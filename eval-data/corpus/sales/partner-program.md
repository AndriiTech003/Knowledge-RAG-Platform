# Northwind Partner Program Guide

**Program owner:** Partner team, reporting to Tom Lindqvist (VP Sales)
**Program year:** 1 January – 31 December 2026
**Systems:** Salesforce partner portal (deal registration), PartnerStack (payouts)

The Northwind Partner Program helps consultancies, system integrators and resellers sell and implement Atlas and Beacon. This guide is for Northwind sales staff working with partners.

## Partner tiers

| Tier | Requirements per year | Benefit | Deal registration protection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Registered | Signed partner agreement | Referral fee of 10% of first-year subscription value | 90 days |
| Silver | 2 certified consultants, 3 closed deals | Resale margin of 20% | 120 days |
| Gold | 5 certified consultants, 8 closed deals, 1 joint case study | Resale margin of 30%, co-marketing funds, dedicated partner manager | 180 days |

Tier status is reviewed every January based on the previous year's results. As of March 2026 the program has 38 Registered, 11 Silver and 4 Gold partners.

## Deal registration

1. The partner registers the opportunity in the Salesforce partner portal with the customer name, contact, and expected products.
2. The Partner Manager approves or rejects the registration within 2 business days. A registration is rejected if an open opportunity for the same customer already exists.
3. Approved registrations are protected for the period shown in the tier table. Protection can be extended once by 60 days if the deal is at Stage 3 or later.

## Referral fees and margins

- Referral fees are paid through PartnerStack 45 days after the customer's first payment.
- Referral fees apply only to the first year of the subscription, not to renewals.
- Resale margins apply to resold contracts for the full contract term.
- Partner margins are not customer discounts and do not count toward the Discount Approval Matrix.

## Certification

Partners certify through the Atlas Academy: "Atlas Certified Implementer" (8 hours) and "Beacon Certified Specialist" (4 hours). Certifications are valid for 2 years.

## Working with partners: rules for Northwind sellers

- Partner-sourced deals stay with the territory AE, who co-sells with the partner.
- Do not share internal documents such as battlecards or pricing approval rules with partners.
- Joint customer calls must include the AE; partners cannot quote prices on Northwind's behalf unless they are Silver or Gold resellers.
- Conflicts between partners on the same account are decided by the Partner Manager and the regional Sales Director.
